## About

I'm not a developer. I'm a regular person with limited skills who builds things by describing them to an AI and fixing whatever breaks.

I'm here to practice, to make small things, and to get a little better each time.

## How I work

1. I say what I want in plain words.
2. I run what comes back.
3. I fix what breaks, usually by asking again.
4. I keep what works and throw away the rest.
